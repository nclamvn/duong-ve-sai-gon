> **License tiers and names**
> - Standard License: included with every pack, "Included with every purchase"
> - Commercial License: per project, "Commercial License PER PROJECT"
> - AI Usage Permit: annual, "Annual term, renewable"; it supplements a Standard or Commercial License
> - Enterprise License: custom pricing, "Custom quote."
> 
> **Price and user thresholds**
> - Standard: "Up to 1M end users / $1M revenue"
> - Commercial: "Revenue up to $30M" and "Projects exceeding 1M end users"
> - Enterprise: "Revenue exceeding $30M" and "Platforms with 10M+ active users"
> - Additional Usage License: "Add an Additional Usage License ($399)"
> 
> **Number of projects**
> - Standard: "Unlimited projects"
> - Commercial: "One license per project." Sequels, new seasons, and major new versions each need their own license.
> 
> **Seats**
> - Enterprise: "Multiple developer seats"
> - The page doesn't list seat counts for other tiers.
> 
> **Game engines**
> - The page doesn't set engine-specific terms. It says third-party store purchases "are subject to both the platform's terms AND MoCap Online's license."
> 
> **Redistribution of animation files**
> - FAQ: "you can't resell or redistribute the raw animation files."
> - AI Permit: "No redistribution of raw data"
> - Standard: "No transfer to clients"
> 
> **Web or real-time use**
> - The page doesn't mention web use. The Standard License FAQ lists "games, apps, videos, VR experiences, archviz, and more" as covered commercial uses, but it doesn't address real-time or web deployment explicitly.
