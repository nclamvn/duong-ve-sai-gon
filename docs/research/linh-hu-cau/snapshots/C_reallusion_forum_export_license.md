> **Export License and game engine use (from the page):**
> 
> - Standard (iContent) License: "**iContent License**: Video or image rendering using iClone"
> - Export License: "**Export License**: Export content to other CG or game tools"
> - Exporting: "If the 3D Content (mesh, motion) you have purchased contains an Export License"
> - Games and apps: "You may then embed the converted content into games and applications"
> - Public distribution limit: "These 3D Content must not be available for public download or use"
> - Resale: "Reallusion content itself may not be repurposed and resold in an external content library"
> - Upgrading: "you will need to purchase the export license version of the content pack to be able to export."
> 
> **Standard vs. Export:** The standard license covers rendered output. The page says, "You are authorized to use any rendered image or video output containing models within iClone and/or Character Creator." Exporting 3D files requires the Export License.
> 
> **Unity/Unreal:** The page does not specifically address using iClone motions in Unity or Unreal. It only states the general export and embedding terms above.
