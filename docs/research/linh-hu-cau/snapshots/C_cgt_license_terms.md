> **Royalty Free License (Sections 21A and 21B, Terms of Use for 3D Models)**
> 
> **Permitted uses (games and commercial)**
> - Buyers can use Products as "Incorporated Product," meaning the Product stays embedded in a larger work and can't be separated out for reuse. Section 21A.2 gives examples, including "as rendered still images or moving images; resold as part of a feature film, broadcast, or stock photography;" and "as purchased by a game's creators as part of a game."
> - Section 21A.4 grants Buyers a non-exclusive, worldwide license to reproduce, modify, and commercially exploit the Product, provided it's used as Incorporated Product.
> 
> **Restrictions**
> - **Redistribution and resale:** Section 21A.6 states that "The resale or redistribution by the Buyer of any Product, obtained from the Site is expressly prohibited" unless it is Incorporated Product.
> - **Sale in original form:** Under section 21A.1, Products can't be sold or given to others in their downloaded form or as 3D-printed physical objects.
> - **Extraction from games:** Section 21A.3 requires that, in software such as video games, "you must take all commercially reasonable measures to prevent the end user from gaining access to the Product," such as using proprietary formats or encryption.
> - **AI training:** Section 21A.2 lists "to machine learning or training of neural network models, including generative AI" as an approved use under the standard Royalty Free License. Section 21B ("Royalty Free License, No AI") removes this right and states that "Product use for machine learning or training of neural network models, including generative AI models, is not permitted." Subscribers always receive the No AI version (section 19A.19).
> 
> **Editorial license difference (Section 22)**
> - Editorial license applies only to Products marked "editorial" or "editorial license," and it adds restrictions on top of the Royalty Free License.
> - Those Products may be used only for editorial purposes with journalistic, cultural, or newsworthy value, such as news programs or news-related websites.
> - Section 22.2 notes that these Products may include material that the rights holders haven't released.
