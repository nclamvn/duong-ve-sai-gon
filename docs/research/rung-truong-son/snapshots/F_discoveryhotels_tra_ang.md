"Tra Ang is the section of the 20-Quyet Thang Road from Km12 to Km14."
"It is a unique traffic route precariously perched on a 400-600m high mountain, overlooking the deep Tra Ang abyss."
"Tra Ang Bridge spans the stream from which it takes its name, set amidst a wild and awe-inspiring natural landscape."
"From 1964 to 1972, this route endured relentless bombing by American planes"
"It was bombarded continuously for 87 days and nights"
"with some days seeing 27 B52s and 30 other planes dropping various types of bombs"
