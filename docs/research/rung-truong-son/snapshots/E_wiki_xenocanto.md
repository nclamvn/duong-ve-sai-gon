"xeno-canto is a citizen science project and repository in which volunteers record, upload and annotate recordings of birds, orthoptera, bats, frogs and land mammals."

"Since it began in 2005, it has collected over 1,000,000 sound recordings from more than 12,900 species worldwide"

"All the recordings are published under one of the Creative Commons licenses, including some with open licences."

"The Creative Commons licenses implemented by the website promote sharing."

"Commercial: No"

"containing about 360,000 recordings of about 9,750 bird species"

"There are about 1,000 missing species, and for many species, there are only a few recordings"
