"This is the natural vegetation cover on land above 600 m elevation, on soil with a low sand content."
"In valleys or flat land it has the same five 5 layer structure as lowland evergreen forest,"
"on steep slopes and ridges there are no emergents and therefore has only four layers."
"On high mountain tops where the soil layer is thin and there is strong sunlight and winds, plant diversity is low"
"In these areas the forest only has three layers and is dominated by the family Poaceae"
"and the species Miscanthus floridulus, Thysanoloena maxima and Sinarundinaria griffithiana."
"In areas with less harsh conditions, members of the family Fagaceae account for up to 60% of the population"
"and trees like Podocarpus neriifolius, Dacrycarpus imbricatus, Podocarpus pilgeri are found."
"Symingtonia populnea, Sinarundinaria griffithiana, Michelia faveolata, Paramichella baillonii,"
"Madhuca pasquieri and Paphiopedilum amabile."
"On limestone karst, vegetation cover is distinctly different to that growing on soils."
"There is only a very thin soil layer on these hills"
"it has a more open canopy and trees are shorter in stature."
"Typically, the forest is dominated by trees of 10-15 metres in height and 50 cm in diameter."
"Karst vegetation is dominated by species which can tolerate dry soil conditions"
"and species which can grow on bare rock."
"forest with a structure unaltered since 1975 is classified as rich forest"

"The average annual temperature is 24-25o C, however monthly temperature variation is considerable."
"December and January are the coolest months when temperatures drop to 20"
"sometimes as low as 15 in areas above 500 m elevation."
"In contrast, in June and July temperatures average 29 and regularly reach 39 when the Phon is blowing."
"The nature reserve also receives the "Phon", a warm dry wind originating in Laos, during the months of March-June."
"Annual rainfall totals for Bac Huong Hoa Nature Reserve are between 2,400 and 2,800 mm."
"There are two main rainy seasons, the first lasts from August to November"
"and accounts for nearly 50% of the annual rainfall."
"The second rainy season lasts from April to August and is characterised by less heavy rain."
"Between February and March there is light rain and December to January is the driest period."
"In June and July the Phon causes the humidity to decrease, sometimes to less than 30%."
"All forest in Bac Huong Hoa Nature Reserve has been affected to some degree by logging,"
"shifting cultivation and wars, especially through the use of chemical defoliants."
"it was affected by aerial spraying of chemical defoliants during the American War."
"On land which received heavy spraying with chemical defoliants and subsequent repeated burning,"
"The effects of defoliant spraying and bombing can be seen clearly in satellite images"
"of central highland provinces taken in 1969."
"it is not conclusive as to whether or not the present forest conditions can be attributed to the use of defoliants."
"At elevations of 700-1200 metres, degraded land is colonised by the large bamboo species Arundiunria peteloti."
"It reaches 3-5 metres in height and grows in dense stands."
"Interspersed with the bamboo are small trees from the families Fagaceae, Theaceae, Styraceae,"
"Aquifoliaceae, Eleocarpaceae, Magnoliaceae and Aeraceae."
