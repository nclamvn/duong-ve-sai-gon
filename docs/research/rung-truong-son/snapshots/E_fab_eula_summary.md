The page is a non-binding summary of the Fab Standard License and points to the full EULA, which is the binding text. It also links to Creative Commons CC-BY terms for assets under that license.

- **Permitted:** Using assets commercially or privately, modifying them to fit your Projects, distributing Projects that include them, and sharing them with collaborators. The page notes that usage "is not limited to Unreal Engine."
- **Prohibited:** Reselling or redistributing an asset "for free on a standalone basis," or letting others do so.
- **Tiers:** Personal and Professional grant the same rights. Reference-Only grants access to the asset in referenced form, not its source format. Moving from Personal to Professional after crossing a revenue threshold does not require an upgrade.
- **Other terms:** No warranties are given, and you don't have to credit the publisher.
