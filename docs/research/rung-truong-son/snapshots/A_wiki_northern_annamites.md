"Rainfall is somewhat less than the lowland rainforest of the lower elevations in Vietnam"

"and the temperatures slightly cooler due to the higher elevation."

"The climate of the ecoregion is Dry-winter humid subtropical climate"

"Precipitation in the wet summer months is ten time or more the average of the winter months."

"Precipitation in the ecoregion varies from 1,500-2,500 mm/year."

"About 75% of the region is forested (about 35,000 km2), most of which is broadleaf evergreen closed forest."

"Lower montane forests, from 800 to 1,200 meters elevation, are dominated by broadleaf evergreen trees"

"The trees form a dense two-tiered canopy 15 to 25 meters tall."

"Epiphytes, particularly orchids, grow abundantly on the trees, and include many endemic species of orchids."

"Understory vegetation is typically sparse in undisturbed forest."

"Thickets of bamboo, including species of Chimonobambusa and Dendrocalamus, grow in disturbed areas"

"Pine forests grow in drier areas and areas with thin or rocky soils up to 1,500 meters elevation."

"Khasi pine (Pinus kesiya) is the dominant species, and can grow 20 to 30 meters tall."

"The pine forests are prone to frequent fires, which help prevent the establishment of broadleaf canopy trees."

"Open-canopied forests dominated by Keteleeria evelyniana are found in northern Laos."

"Upper montane forests, from 1,200 to 1,800 meters elevation with a cool moist character, are dominated by conifers"

"Fokienia trees can reach 40 to 50 meters tall."
