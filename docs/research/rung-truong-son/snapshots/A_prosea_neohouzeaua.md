Vietnam: cai noa, kei noua.

Widely distributed from Bangladesh to Vietnam.

Culm 7-9 m tall, erect or leaning to nearby vegetation, slender, diameter up to 1 cm, thin walled;

internodes 40-60 cm long; nodes slightly swollen.

Open tufted bamboo.

Usually found wild in evergreen forest at 300-700 m altitude, but commonly planted in villages.
