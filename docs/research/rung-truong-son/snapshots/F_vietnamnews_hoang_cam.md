"A fire stove dug into a hill's foot or on the ground, with the smoke channels like a squid's tentacles."
"Cầm started working on channelling the smoke into a system of tunnels like a rat's cave."
"He dug dozens, with many tunnels that spread the smoke around the ground surface."
"He would place tree branches on the tunnels and then cover them with dirt so no visible smoke could come out."
"The channels were then covered with tree branches and damp dirt on top."
"He also dug a large hole in front of the stove, covered with a plastic mat"
"Underneath, the hole was like a smoke cell, giving more air for the fire to burn stronger."
"Smoke from the furnace spread out into the channels and was screened and blocked by the ground."
"It rose but looked just like the morning mist and quickly vanished."
"In October 1952, the army gave this new kitchenette a name: the Hoàng Cầm stove, after its inventor."
