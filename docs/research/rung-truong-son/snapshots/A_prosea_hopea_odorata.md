"Vietnam: sao den, sao bã mía, sao cát."

"Laos: (maiz) kh'è:n, kh'èn h'üa"

"Bangladesh, Burma, Laos, southern Vietnam, Cambodia, Thailand, the Andaman Islands and northern Peninsular Malaysia."

"A medium-sized to large tree of up to 45 m tall"

"bole straight, cylindrical, branchless for up to 25 m, with a diameter of up to 120 cm and prominent buttresses"

"bark surface scaly, dark brown, outer bark rather thick, inner bark dull yellow, tinged green at the cambium"

"H. odorata is a riparian species and usually occurs on deep rich soils up to 600 m altitude."
