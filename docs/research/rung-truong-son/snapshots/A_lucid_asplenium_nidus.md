"Ferns with nest-like habit. Rhizome erect, scaly, unbranched with a single growing point."

"Fronds spreading horizontally before arising in a regular rosette."

"Rachis in cross-section prominent and often 2-angled on upper surface, flat or only slightly keeled below."

"Lamina narrowly obovate, simple, 90–125 cm long, 8.5–15 cm wide, shiny green and ±glabrous"

"1b. Midrib rounded on lower surface = Asplenium nidus"

"This is a large nest-forming species with a rosette of fronds that funnel rain and falling debris into its root system."

"Epiphytic or lithophytic in high to low rainfall vine forest."

"It is an abundant canopy epiphyte in low altitude high rainfall rainforest and gallery forest"

"an abundant lithophyte on rocks, especially in boulder pile vine forest."

"Torres Strait to the Whitsundays NE QLD. Also widespread in the Palaeotropics."
