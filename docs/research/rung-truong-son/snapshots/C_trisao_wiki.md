"Crested arguses have dark-brown-spotted black and buff plumage"

"a heavy pink bill, brown irises and blue skin around the eyes"

"The male has a broad and greatly elongated tail of twelve feathers."

"The tail coverts measure up to 1.73 m (5.7 ft) in length, giving the bird a total length of 1.9–2.39 m (6.2–7.8 ft)."

"The female is similar in size, with a prominent marbled barring and more colourful dorsal plumage than the male."

"Shy and elusive, the crested argus is found in submontane primary forests of Vietnam, Laos and Malaysia in Southeast Asia."

"nest sites in elevated reaches, often amongst escarpments on steep slopes amongst leaf litter"

"The male performs various displays that include serpentine erratic deportment behaviors"

"Like other peafowl, crested argus retire on emergent trees above the forest canopy for many hours a day."

"The two forms differ significantly in plumage, display and vocalisations and both are threatened by human activity."
