"This species of Alocasia grows to about 0.5–1.6 m high, or just over 5 feet, with corms measuring 4 cm"

"The leaves are big, diamond-blade-shaped, slightly “teardrop” in form, but ovate"

"The petioles are 0.3–1.0 m long, with the lower parts clasped around the stem."

"native to East and Southeast Asia (Japan, China, Indochina, Assam, Bangladesh, Borneo, Taiwan)."

"Traditionally, A. odora is sometime used as a medicine for the treatment of the common cold in Vietnam."

"The leaves and stems should not be consumed raw, nor should any of the green plant material."

"the plant is actually inedible when raw, and may cause mild to significant gastrointestinal discomfort"

"Consumption of undercooked aroid leaves can result in several uncomfortable symptoms"

"As with the green plant material, the corm should also not be consumed raw or undercooked."
