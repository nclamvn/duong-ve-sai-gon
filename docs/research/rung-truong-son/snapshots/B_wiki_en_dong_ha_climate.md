"Đông Hà has a tropical monsoon climate"
"There is a wet season due to the northeast monsoon between August and December, peaking in October"
"with around 629.7 millimetres or 25 inches of rain and frequent typhoon landfalls."
"The dry season lasts from January to July, though there is a secondary rainfall peak in May."
"The highest temperature recorded was 44.0 °C on 28 April 2024, while the lowest was 9.4 °C on 2 March 1986."
