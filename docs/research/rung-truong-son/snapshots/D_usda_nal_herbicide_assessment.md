"Orange acts as both a defoliant and a systemic plant killer on broad leafed and woody vegetation."

"leaf fall occurs in three to six weeks after application, with surviving trees or branches refoliating within a year."

"An increase in grass cover was noted in sprayed areas and it was anticipated that bamboo also would increase."

"a single spraying causes 10 to 20 percent killing of merchantable trees"

"two treatments in successive years kill 50 to 100 percent in the type of forest studied."

"mangrove species are killed by a single treatment"

"sprayed mangrove forests might return to their original condition in approximately twenty years."

"a rather sharply defined swath of affected vegetation approximately 85 meters wide and 15 kilometers long"

"some areas have been treated more than once."

"the objective of spraying a forest is simply to remove as much cover as possible."
