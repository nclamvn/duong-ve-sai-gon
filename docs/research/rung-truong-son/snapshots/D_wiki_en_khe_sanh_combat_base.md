"Khe Sanh landscape."

"on the Xom Cham Plateau"

"Militarily, it was on relatively level ground and offered good fields of fire in all directions."

"The terrain provided both good drainage and stable soil, mostly consisting of"

"laterite clay or weathered iron/aluminum rock."

"It also contained a few basalt outcroppings, at what was later called the "Rock Quarry"."

"Elevation: 1,608 feet (490 m) AMSL"

"On 30 January 1971, Khe Sanh was reactivated by the U.S. Army (Operation Dewey Canyon II) to support"

"Operation Lam Son 719, the South Vietnamese invasion of Laos."

"Since its abandonment, most of the base has become overgrown by wilderness or coffee and banana plants."
