"Laos: ph'ông, lau"

"Vietnam: co' lách."

"Rhizomatous perennial, with culms 1-4 m tall or more, waxy below the nodes."

"Leaf-sheath tight, 20 cm or more long, overlapping, striate, often purplish, glabrous;"

"leaf-blade linear-acuminate, 50-90 cm × 5-15(-40) mm, glabrous, margins scaberulous."

"S. spontaneum grows from near sea-level up to 1700 m altitude."

"Large tussocks will form when grown on river banks or ditches, thereby colonizing large areas."

"from alluvial soils on river banks to sandy soils of old mines."
