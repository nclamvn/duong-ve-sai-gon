The EULA has no passages on real-time applications, game engines, interactive applications, extended or game licenses, or distribution of models within products. It mentions games only in the sections below. Each quote is under the 125-character limit.

Standard Licence § 2(8)(3):
"to create computer games and software"

Standard Licence § 2(3):
"excluding any resale, sharing, sublicensing, distribution or reproduction"

Standard Licence § 3(8):
"The above provision does not refer to games."

Professional Licence § 2(8)(3):
"to create computer games and software"

Professional Licence § 4(9):
"The above provision does not refer to games."

Restriction, Standard Licence § 3(1) and Professional Licence § 4(1):
"The Licensee shall not resell, give, share (whether for remuneration or not), sublicence, distribute"
