Vietnam: chò nâu.

"India (Assam), Burma, Laos, Cambodia, Vietnam, Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra, Java and the Lesser Sunda Islands"

"A medium-sized to large tree of up to 48 m tall"

"bole cylindrical, branchless for 15-20 m, up to 150 cm in diameter"

"bark surface slightly fissured, grey"

"Leaves broadly elliptical-oblong, 16-28(-50) cm × 7-17(-20) cm, base obtuse, apex obtuse to emarginate"

"secondary veins 16-19 pairs, ascending, glabrous"

"occurs in moist, evergreen to semi-deciduous montane forest, at 800-1500 m altitude"

"but as low as 100 m in seasonal areas."
