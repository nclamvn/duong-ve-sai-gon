"The Phong Nha–Kẻ Bàng karst has evolved since the Paleozoic (some 400 million years ago),"

"making it the oldest major karst area in Asia."

"It has been subject to massive tectonic changes, and comprises a multitude of rock types"

"As many as seven different major levels of karst development have occurred"

"Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng National Park is one of the world's two largest limestone regions."

"Limestone in the Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng area has been exploited for commercial purposes by local inhabitants"

"The Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng area today is the result of five stages of Earth's crust development and movement:"

"Karstic landforms in this area are of typical tropical karst which are divided into two groups of forms"

"Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng contains two dozens of mountain peaks with over 1,000 metres in height."

"Noteworthy peaks are the Peak Co Rilata with a height of 1,128 m and the Peak Co Preu with a height of 1,213 m."

"The highest summit in the non-karstic area and also the highest summit of this national park is Co Preu (1213 m),"

"Non-karstic topographical area accounts for a low percentage, distributing mainly in outer circle of limestone"

"with the deep divisions and high sloping level from 25 to 30°."

"There are extreme sloping mountains here with a height of over 1,000 m,"

"Besides the grotto and cave systems, Phong Nha has the longest underground river."

"The Sơn and Chay are the main rivers in this national park."

"Most of caves here have been shaped by Son and Chay Rivers."

"The Son River flows into the mouth of the Phong Nha cave and keeps on underground, where it is called the Nam Aki River."

"Phong Nha Cave, like most of the caves in this area, has been continuously shaped by the Chay River."

"There are spectacular streams, springs and waterfalls in Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng,"

"the climate in Phong Nha–Kẻ Bàng national park is tropically hot and humid."

"The annual mean temperature is 23–25 °C (73–77 °F),"

"with a maximum of 41 °C (106 °F) in the summer"

"The hottest months in this region fall from June to August, with an average temperature of 28 °C (82 °F),"

"and the coldest months from December to February with an average temperature of 18 °C (64 °F)."

"Annual rainfall is about 2,000 mm to 2,500 mm (80–100 in)."

"88% of the rainfall falls during the monsoon, from July to December,"

"some rain still falls throughout the dry season."

"Mean annual relative humidity is 84%."

"By far the largest vegetation type is tropical dense moist evergreen forest on limestone"

"Seedlings can only grow in holes and cracks in the limestone where soil has accumulated,"
