"The great hornbill is a large bird, 95–130 cm (37–51 in) long, with a 152 cm (60 in) wingspan"

"The average weight of males is 3 kg (6.6 lb) whereas that of females is 2.59 kg (5.7 lb)."

"Females are smaller than males and have bluish-white instead of red eyes, although the orbital skin is pinkish."

"The most prominent feature of the hornbill is the bright yellow and black casque on top of its massive bill."

"The casque appears U-shaped when viewed from the front, and the top is concave, with two ridges along the sides"

"The wing beats are heavy, and the sound produced by birds in flight can be heard from a distance."

"This sound has been likened to the puffing of a steam locomotive starting up."

"They make loud duets, beginning with a loud "kok" given about once a second by the male, to which the female joins in."

"The pair then calls in unison, turning into a rapid mixture of roars and barks."

"Great hornbills are usually seen in small parties, with larger groups sometimes aggregating at fruit trees."

"They form monogamous pair bonds and live in small groups of 2-40 individuals."

"A congregation of 150 to 200 birds has been recorded in southeastern Bhutan."

"The great hornbill is native to the forests of India, Bhutan, Nepal, Mainland Southeast Asia,"

"Its distribution is fragmented in the Western Ghats and in the foothills of the Himalayas."

"It prefers dense old growth unlogged forests in hilly regions."

"It appears to be dependent on large stretches of rain forests."
