"Khe Sanh" is the commune of Quảng Trị Province, Vietnam, located 63 km west of Đông Hà."

"During the Vietnam War, the Khe Sanh Combat Base was located to the north of the city."

"Most of the former base is now overgrown by wilderness or coffee and banana plants."
