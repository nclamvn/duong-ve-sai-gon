"These annelids are known from subtropical and tropical regions around the Indian and Pacific Ocean."

"Members of the genus feed on blood."

"They are troublesome to humans and animals especially because their bites result in prolonged bleeding."

"Haemadipsa sylvestris Blanchard, 1894 - India, Indochina, Malesia"
