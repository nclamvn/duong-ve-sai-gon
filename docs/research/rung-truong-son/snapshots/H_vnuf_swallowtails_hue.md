"Total 36 species of the Papilionidae family has been recorded in three protected areas."

"Bach Ma National Park (NP), Phong Dien Nature Reserve (NR), and Sao La Nature Reserve (NR)"

"at different habitats and altitudes in Bach Ma National Park (NP)"

"There are 4 species listed in the Red Data Book of Vietnam (2007)"

"Troides helena, T. aeacus, Teinopalpus aureus, and Papilio noblei."

"Teinopalpus aureus is in the Red List of IUCN (2018)."

"Bach Ma NP has 7 species not found in two other protected areas."

"Bach Ma NP has high conservation value of butterfly species"
