"They have a familiar deep resonant call which is associated with omens in many parts of its range."

"The calls are a booming low coop-coop-coops repeated and with variations and some duets between individuals."

"Other calls include a rapid rattling" and "a harsh scolding" and "a hissing threat call."

"They are large, crow-like with a long tail and coppery brown wings"

"The head is black, upper mantle and underside are black glossed with purple."

"The back and wings are chestnut brown."

"The eyes are ruby red."

"This is a large species of cuckoo at 48 cm."

"The sexes are similar in plumage but females are slightly larger."

"found in a wide range of habitats from jungle to cultivation and urban gardens."

"They are weak fliers, and are often seen clambering about in vegetation or walking on the ground"

"They sunbathe in the mornings singly or in pairs on the top of vegetation with their wings spread out."

"They are most active in the warm hours of the morning and in the late afternoon."

"A widespread resident in the Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia"

"The nominate race is found from the Indus Valley through the sub-Himalayan and Gangetic plains to Nepal"
