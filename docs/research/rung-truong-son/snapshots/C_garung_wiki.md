"The whole bird may be as long as 70 centimetres (28 in)."

"Compared with the domestic chicken, the red junglefowl has a much smaller body mass."

"The plumage of the male is much brighter in colouration than that of the female"

"The mantle (neck and back) of the rooster typically has long, golden hackle feathers."

"The tail consists of 14 iridescent feathers that shimmer with blue, purple, and green in direct light."

"Hens lack the long, ornate feathers that are a prominent feature of the roosters."

"Male red junglefowl have a shorter crowing sound than domestic roosters; the call cuts off abruptly at the end."

"The crowing of roosters is controlled by their circadian clock."

"The top-ranking rooster always crows first, followed by its subordinates, in descending order of social rank."

"Red junglefowl typically live in flocks of one to a few males and several females."

"Males are more likely to occur alone than females."

"Flight in these birds is almost purely confined to reaching their roosting areas at sunset in trees"

"Red junglefowl prefer disturbed habitats and edges, both natural and human-created."

"found across much of Southeast Asia and parts of South Asia."
