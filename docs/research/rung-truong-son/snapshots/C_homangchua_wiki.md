"Adult king cobras are 3.18 to 4 m (10.4 to 13.1 ft) long."

"The longest known individual measured 5.85 m (19.2 ft)."

"The king cobra's skin is olive green with black and white bands on the trunk that converge to the head."

"Juveniles are black with chevron shaped white, yellow or buff bars that point towards the head."

"Its hood is oval shaped and covered with olive green smooth scales and two black spots between the two lowest scales."

"The hiss of the king cobra is a much lower pitch than many other snakes"

"king cobra growls consist solely of frequencies below 2,500 Hz, with a dominant frequency near 600 Hz"

"When alarmed, it raises the front part of its body, extends the hood, shows the fangs and hisses loudly."

"It is considered diurnal because it hunts during the day, but has also been seen at night, albeit rarely."

"The king cobra has a wide distribution throughout tropical Asia."

"down to Bangladesh, Myanmar, southern China, Cambodia, Thailand, Laos, Vietnam;"
