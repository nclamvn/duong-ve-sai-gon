"is a mountain pass in the Annamite Range between northern Vietnam and Laos"
"located approximately 60km southwest of Đồng Hới and 115km northwest of Quảng Trị, Vietnam."
"connects National Road 565 (formerly Route 137) in Vietnam to Route 912 in Khammouane Province in Laos."
"one of the principal entry points into the Ho Chi Minh trail through Laos."
"From 19 April to 24 June 1968 the USAF conducted Operation Turnpike, an intensive air interdiction campaign"
"that included the use of B-52 bombers."
"over a four square mile area on an approach road to the pass."
"During 1968 the area around the pass was defended by numerous machine-guns"
"In December 1970 the North Vietnamese stationed SA-2 missiles"
"During 1970 the North Vietnamese built a petroleum-oil-lubricants pipeline through the pass."
