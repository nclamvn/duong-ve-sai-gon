"Adult males measure about 53 cm (21 in) on average and weigh about 7.7 kg (17 lb)."
"The rhesus macaque is brown or grey in color and has a pink face, which is bereft of fur."
"Like other macaques, rhesus troops comprise a mixture of 20–200 males and females."
"The rhesus macaque is diurnal, arboreal, and terrestrial."
"When in threatening situations, macaques emit a single loud, high-pitched sound called a shrill bark."
