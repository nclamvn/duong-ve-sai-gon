Paraphrased, not quoted: The document names a Hà Tĩnh langur population and a black langur it says may be a separate species, though the authors doubt that identification. It also reports a rare babbler rediscovered in Laos and later in the area, and a Hà Tĩnh-area gibbon-type primate is not mentioned. Several bird species with narrow ranges are noted, and the area is treated as part of an important lowland endemic bird region. Hunting is described as widespread, commercial, and in some cases unsustainable, with snares used widely and some large mammals at risk of local extinction. The red-shanked douc is said to be probably in sharp decline, and the document gives no overall species count.
