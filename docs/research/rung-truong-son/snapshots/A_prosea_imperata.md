"Vietnam: cỏ tranh."

"Laos: hnha:z kh'a:"

"Culms erect, 0.1—1.2(—3 m) tall, 1—4(—8)-noded, unbranched, solid, usually hairy at the nodes."

"leaf-blade linear-lanceolate, 10—180 cm x 5—25 mm, flat, erect, spreading or drooping, pilose at the base,"

"when old with hard, serrate, cutting edges and a stout whitish midrib."

"at the base with a dense whorl of silky white hairs up to 2.5 cm long"

"Aggressively rhizomatous, robust perennial with white, deeply buried (up to more than 1 m), branched,"

"fleshy, scaly rhizome, forming loose to compact tufts of leaves."

"The rhizomes are highly competitive and penetrate the roots of other plants, causing rot or death."

"Imperata cylindrica is favoured by burning, which can lead to virtually monospecific swards."

"Its resistance to burning is associated with its vigorous underground rhizomes, but seedlings also establish after burning."

"it has become widely spread through man's intervention, particularly following slashing and burning of forest lands."

"It grows in grassland, cultivated areas, and plantations."

"It grows at altitudes from sea-level up to 2000 m in several countries and has been recorded at 2700 m in Indonesia."
