**Title:** "Newly recorded corticolous lichens for Vietnam"

**Study area:** Urban sites in Ho Chi Minh City and forested sites in Lam Dong Province in the Central Highlands.

"They recognized 275 species and estimated 1.000 species in total to occur."
"which resulted in 189 new records for the country and 11 new species for science"
"Thallus crustose to subfoliose, approximately 1 cm in diameter, cushion-shaped,"
"Thallus foliose, corticate, wrinkled on both surfaces, gelatinous and swelling when wet, grey,"
"Thallus immersed to the substrate, greyish to yellowish or pale green, smooth, ecorticate, thin."
"The specimens from Vietnam were collected on exposed bark in semi-dry to dry habitats."
"The Vietnamese material was found on a twig in the evergreen broad-leaved forest mixed with conifers,"
"We found this species growing on an exposed branch in the secondary forest"
"The Vietnamese specimens are observed on tree trunks in a couple of plantations at an altitude of 03–33 m."
