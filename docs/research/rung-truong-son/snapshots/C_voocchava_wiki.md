"The red-shanked douc are among the most colorful primates."

"Their crown is silver, their temples are red hair and their foreheads are black."

"Their collar is orange with black from one shoulder to the other."

"Their face is a yellow-brown color and they have light blue eyelids."

"The male has a head and body length of 55–82 centimetres (22–32 in)"

"Males weigh 8.6–11.4 kilograms (19–25 lb), and females 6.6–10.5 kilograms (15–23 lb)."

"Although minor, the red-shanked doucs are sexually dimorphic with respect to body size but not coloration."

"The red-shanked douc is native to Indochina; Vietnam, southern Laos, and possibly northeastern Cambodia,"

"In Laos, red-shanked doucs occur from the southern Nam Ghong Provincial Protected Area"

"In Vietnam, the species lives between Nghệ An and Gia Lai provinces, from 18°29'N to 14°21'N."

"Red-shanked doucs are found in evergreen, semi-evergreen and occasionally limestone forests,"

"up to 1,600 metres (5,200 ft) in elevation."

"The bands contain a mean of 18 individuals in a mean of 2.7 units per band and a mean of 6.5 individuals per unit."

"Groups of up to 50 have been recorded."

"The activity budget of wild red-shanked doucs is rest at 35.3%, movement at 28.6%, socializing at 21.7%,"

"feeding at 13.7% and self-grooming at 0.7%."

"They are arboreal and spend most of their time in the forest canopy, including to sleep."

"This douc is an aerial specialist, moving high up in the canopy."

"The red-shanked douc has a low-pitched growl that is given as a threat, and a short, harsh distress squeal."

"When startled, they may bark loudly and slap branches with their limbs."

"In 2020, the IUCN updated their classification of the species to critically endangered,"

"The main threat to the red-shanked douc is from hunting."

"More than half of the species has been lost in the past 30–40 years."
