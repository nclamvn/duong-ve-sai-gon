"The list of bamboo species in Vietnam includes 210 species/subspecies belonging to 27 genera."

"some new genera for Vietnam were found, such as Maclurochloa with 16 species, Melocalamus with 9 species"

"Some genera have many species, such as Bambusa with 62 species/subspecies, Dendrocalamus with 29 species/subspecies"

"Gigantochloa with 17 species, Schizostachyum with 13 species and Indosasa with 11 species"
