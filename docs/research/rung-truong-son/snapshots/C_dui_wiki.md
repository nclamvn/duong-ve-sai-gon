"The hoary bamboo rat is a robust rodent that reaches a head-and-body length of 240 to 345 mm (9 to 14 in)"

"Its weight ranges from 1,500 to 2,500 g (53 to 88 oz)."

"The fur on the upper parts is greyish-brown or dark brown"

"lives alone in a simple burrow, which has a single entrance marked by a mound,"

"shrubby land bordering woodland, plantations, and bamboo thickets."

"The bamboo rat emerges at night to forage for plant material,"

"eastern Myanmar, Thailand, Laos, Cambodia, Vietnam, the Malay Peninsula,"
