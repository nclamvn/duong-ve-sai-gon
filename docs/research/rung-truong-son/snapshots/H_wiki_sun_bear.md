"The head-and-body length is between 100 and 140 cm (39 and 55 in), and the shoulder height is nearly 70 cm (28 in)."
"Adults weigh 25–65 kg (55–143 lb)."
"The fur is generally jet black, but can vary from grey to red."
"varies from orange or ochre-yellow to buff or cream, or even white."
"The snout is grey, silver, or orange."
"The claws are sickle-shaped; the front claws are long and heavy."
"It is mainly active during the day, though nocturnality might be more common in areas frequented by humans."
"It is an excellent climber; it sunbathes or sleeps in trees 2 to 7 m (6 ft 7 in to 23 ft 0 in) above the ground."
"Bedding sites consist mainly of fallen hollow logs, but they also rest in standing trees with cavities,"
"Distinguishing Asiatic black bears and sun bears by claw marks on climbed trees"
"roars similar to those of a male orangutan during the breeding season"
"kept in bear farms in Laos, Vietnam, and Myanmar."
"populations in Vietnam are feared to decline severely by 50–80% in the next 30 years."
