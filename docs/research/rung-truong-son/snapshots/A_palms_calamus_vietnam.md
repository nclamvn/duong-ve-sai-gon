New Species of Calamus (Palmae) from Vietnam

"Calamus is the largest genus of palms, currently thought to contain 374 species"
"Evans et al. recognize 18 species from Vietnam"
"Known only from Phu Yen in central Vietnam, on steep rocky slopes in disturbed forest."
"in lowland forest on limestone soils at low elevations."
"on steep slopes in lowland rain forest at 500–800 m elevation."
"in primary, broad-leaved, closed, evergreen, wet, mountain forest on steep sandstone slopes at 1100–1200 m elevation."
"Stems clustered, climbing, to 10 m tall, 1.3 cm diameter with sheaths."
"Stems solitary, to 70 m long, 4–5.5 cm diameter with sheaths."
"with scattered, green, downward-pointing spines to 1 cm long, with crescent-shaped, concave bases;"
"flagella to 5 m long"
