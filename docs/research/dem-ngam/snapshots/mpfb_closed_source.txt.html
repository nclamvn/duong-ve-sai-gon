Can I use models made with MPFB in a closed-source game? :: MakeHuman Community MakeHuman Community  >  MPFB  >  MPFB FAQ  >  Can I use models made with MPFB in a closed-source game? Can I use models made with MPFB in a closed-source game? Yes. All core assets (the base mesh, targets, skins…) are shared under CC0 . Note that if you use a third party asset shared under a different license it is your responsibility to fulfill the obligations of that license. But that is between you and the author of that asset.
